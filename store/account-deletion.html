Delete your Crux account

This page permanently deletes your Crux account and every row of data tied to it, without needing the app installed. It calls the same deletion the app itself uses in Settings.

This cannot be undone. Your profile, training program, workout history, bodyweight log, measurements, chat history and account sign-in are all deleted immediately. Progress photos live only on your device and are unaffected by this page — clear them from the app or by uninstalling.

Option 1 — from inside the app (fastest)

Open Crux → Profile → Settings → Delete my account.

Option 2 — from this page

Sign in with the email and password for the account you want deleted.

Signed in with Google or Apple, and never set a password? This form can't authenticate you — use Option 1 in the app, or email privacy@crux.app from your account's address and we will verify and delete it manually within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, training program, and every logged workout
  • Bodyweight history and body measurements
  • Water and protein log entries
  • AI Coach chat history
  • Friendships and quest/streak progress
  • Your sign-in identity (email, password hash, OAuth link)

Payment records, where they exist, are kept for the period tax and accounting law requires, with the link to your identity removed. Full detail in the Privacy Policy.
